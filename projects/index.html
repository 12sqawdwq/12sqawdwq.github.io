Robotics and embodied AI

  • FlyHeadactive project

    A testbed for connectome-driven control that connects a simulated whole-brain fruit-fly connectome (FlyWire) to a real wheel-legged robot. The LIF brain model is taken unmodified from eonsystemspbc/fly-brain; as sole developer, built everything around it: sensor encoders, the descending-neuron decoder, GPU acceleration, the safety layer, LiDAR SLAM navigation, a web console and a 3D site, demonstrated on the real robot.
